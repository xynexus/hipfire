## Navigating Content by Design Process

AMD Adaptive Computing documentation is organized around a set of standard design processes to help you find relevant content for your current development task. You can access the AMD Versal™ adaptive SoC design processes on the [Design Hubs](https://docs.amd.com/go/en-US/ug1776-adaptive-computing-design-hubs-lp) page. You can also use the [Design Flow Assistant](https://docs.amd.com/p/versal-decision-tree-welcome) to better understand the design flows and find content that is specific to your intended design needs. This document covers the following design processes:

- **AI Engine Development:** Creating the AI Engine graph and kernels, library use, simulation debugging and profiling, and algorithm development. Also includes the integration of the PL and AI Engine kernels.
