## Setup

- Source: `/srv/hipfire/models/models--Qwen--Qwen3.5-0.8B.hfa`, read **in place**
  (the usage strings claiming a directory is required are wrong).
- Text-only: 752.4M params (embed 254.3M **tied**, body 498.1M). Vision (100.6M)
  and MTP (20.5M) are excluded from every bpw figure below.
- Calibration: `calib-multi-8m` (multilingual), 186 Hessians + 186 imatrix.
- KLD scored against **two held-out** references, both disjoint from calibration:
  `evalA` = wikitext tail of `calib-5m`, `evalB` = a stratified 15% hold-out of the

- `bpw` is **on-disk over 752.4M text params**. On-disk and resident differ; see below.

Q8 embed is free on this model: the `--embed-precision source` default spends a full

"the embed is the largest per-tensor KLD cost" note — true at 4-bit and below, not
true at Q8 here.

### Quality per bit

`B2` at 4.41 bpw has **3.3× better KLD than `C1` at 3.66 bpw**. Buying the last
0.75 bpw costs more than everything the embed lever saved. On a 0.8B model — little
redundancy to spare — 3-bit weights are simply expensive.

A is near-lossless, which is the gate that makes every number below it meaningful:
if oq8++ were not near-lossless the calibration would be suspect.

## The three findings that changed the plan

### 1. The opening analysis was inverted — measured, then corrected

The parameter split says the embed is 29% of the model and prior work says the embed
is the largest per-tensor KLD cost, so this looked like an embedding problem. The
*arithmetic* was right (a bf16 embed alone spends 4068 Mbit against the 3092 Mbit a
4.0 bpw budget allows, so sub-4-bit is impossible without touching it). The *quality*
conclusion was wrong:

- tied embed 8-bit → 4-bit: **+0.039 kld, −1.44 bpw** — nearly free.
- the qtip3 3-bit body alone: **+0.294 kld** — 6.5× the entire oq4.25++ budget.

The embed is the cheap half. Measuring before building (P4 before P5/P6) is the only
reason this was caught; building the qtip3 embed gather first would have spent the
night on the wrong half of the problem.

### 2. On-disk bits ≠ resident bits, and only qtip3 is genuinely low-bit in VRAM

`oq8_arch.rs` widens **OqPlusCompact (qt 36)** and **Oq3 (qt 38)** to `DType::Oq8G256`
at load; the qwen35 loader routes `33 | 35 | 36 | 52 | 54` the same way.

| body format | on-disk | **resident** |
|---|---|---|
| oq4.25++ (OqPlusCompact) | 4.31 b | **8 b** |
| oq3++ | 3.06 b | **8 b** |
| qtip3 (Qtip3G256) | 3.13 b | **3.13 b** (native `gemv_qtip3g256`) |

So "3 bits" meaning a *smaller file* is easy; meaning a *smaller and faster model* is
only deliverable by qtip3. This matters on a bandwidth-bound box.

### 3. qtip3 is GEMV-only — a decode format with a prefill penalty

There is no `gemm_qtip3*` anywhere, and `Qtip3G256` is absent from `gemm_table.rs`.
Batched consumers (prefill, KLD scoring) fall back to a per-token GEMV loop — the same
trap the qwen35 lm_head comment documents for Bf16Lut3. It is why every qtip3 score in
this run took ~4× an oq* score, and it predicts slow prefill.

## Negative results (documented per the goal file)

- **`HIPFIRE_LOWRANK_R` is a no-op on arch 5.** C2 scored identically to C1 to six
  decimals while being 84.2 MB (+0.90 bpw) larger. `lr_u`/`lr_v` are consumed **only**
  by `hipfire-arch-minimax`; the qwen35 loader has zero references. The prior
  "low-rank residual is a key lever (−13%@2b)" note was MiniMax and/or the qtip **sim**
  path, not a general result.
- **`--mixed-bpw` cannot serve a sub-4 target.** Its floor is Oq4 by construction
  ("only the oq4 → oq8 step is available"); it promotes, never demotes.
- **`HIPFIRE_QTIP_CODEBOOK=3inst`** (a better Gaussian codebook) tags output as
  `Qtip3G256I3` (qt 51), and the qwen35 loader has **no qt-51 arm** — the artifact
  would not load. Unused.
- **The qtip3 path force-quantizes the embed to Q8F16** regardless of
  `--embed-precision`, which is why its "bf16 embed" and "q8 embed" arms produced
  byte-identical artifacts. Only the new `hfq4` arm overrides it.

### Throughput — and why the 3-bit artifact loses

| | bpw | kld | decode | prefill |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B2 oq4.25++ + hfq4 | 4.41 | 0.0755 | 194.1 | 2128 |
| C1 qtip3+cond + hfq4 | 3.66 | 0.2469 | 175.0 | 238 |

C1's prefill is **9× slower** (238 vs 2128 tok/s, ttft 117.8 vs 13.2 ms) — the missing
`gemm_qtip3`. And its decode is *lower* than B2's despite being 3.13-bit resident against
B2's 8-bit: the trellis decode ALU cost exceeds the bandwidth it saves. So qtip3's one
architectural advantage does not materialise on gfx1151.

**B2 dominates C1 on every operational axis** — 3.3× better KLD, faster decode, 9× faster
prefill. C1 wins 0.75 bpw of disk and nothing else.

The 4-bit embed is also a **24% decode speedup** (194.1 vs 156.0), not just a size win:
the tied lm_head GEMV reads 135 MB instead of 367 MB.

### 3-bit body: qtip3 vs oq3 at equal size

| body (~3.6 bpw) | evalA kld |
|---|---|
| qtip3 + greedy OBS conditioning | 0.2469 |
| oq3++ (AWQ + LDLQ) | 0.3367 |

qtip3 with conditioning is the better 3-bit body by 27%. `HIPFIRE_QTIP_COND=greedy` is a
free win worth 26% on its own (0.3335 → 0.2469) and was simply never set — it is not a
missing feature, and my first reading that "QTIP-LDLQ is unreachable on the shippable
path" was wrong and is retracted in the run log.

## Recommendation

**B2 (`oq4.25++` + `hfq4` embed) is the artifact to ship**: 4.41 bpw, 415 MB, KLD 0.075,
the fastest decode and prefill measured. It is 2.5× smaller than the existing
`qwen3.5-0.8b--oq4++.hfq` (776 MB) and materially faster.

Sub-4.0 bpw **is** reachable (C1, 3.66 bpw) but costs 3.3× the KLD and 9× the prefill
time. On a 0.8B model there is little redundancy to spend, and 3-bit weights are simply
expensive here.

## P6 SpinQuant — machinery fixed; a random rotation is redundant here

Two real bugs were found and fixed, after which `--rotate` produces a correct model
on arch 5:

| Qwen3.5-0.8B `oq8++` | evalA KLD |
|---|---|
| unrotated (control) | 0.000676 |
| `R1 = I` no-op | 0.000656 |
| Hadamard, before the embed fix | 7.782 |
| Hadamard, after the embed fix | 0.001057 |

**Bug 1 — the embed shipped unrotated.** `source_precision_tensor_bytes(raw, dtype,
_f32_data)` ignores the f32 and returns raw source bytes, so under the default
`--embed-precision source` the embedding was written in the *original* basis while every
reader had been rotated. It seeds the whole residual stream, hence the 7.78.

**Bug 2 — the Hessian was never rotated.** `ldlq.rs`, `hessian_io.rs` and
`ldlq_hessian_for_tensor` had no rotation awareness, so rotated weights were quantized
against calibration statistics in the original activation basis. Fixing it
(`H → R1 H R1ᵀ`, applied to the 138 reader-rotated tensors) is worth **42%** at 4 bits:
oq4.25++ 0.101696 → 0.058662.

### But rotation still does not pay, and that is *not* a contradiction

Decomposing at 4 bits with plain RTN (no Hessian involved at all):

| oq4 plain RTN | KLD | |
|---|---|---|
| unrotated | 0.209132 | — |
| fold, no rotate (`R1 = I`) | 0.231339 | +0.0222 folding |
| fold + rotate (Hadamard) | 0.253637 | +0.0223 rotation |

Folding and rotation each cost ~0.022 independently. A result contradicting QuaRot/QuIP#
should be treated as a bug — but here there is a reconciliation: **hipfire's codecs
already do incoherence processing.** Every Opus/MQ/QTIP format applies a signed FWHT-256
to weights *and* activations. The literature measures gains against codecs with no
built-in rotation; if each 256-group is already randomised, a second *global* Hadamard
adds no incoherence and only costs folding, untying and numerical error — exactly what
was measured.

The decisive confirmation would be a no-FWHT codec, but `Oq8Plain`/`Oq4Plain` have no
CLI token or match arm, so testing it means new codec plumbing. Recorded as
well-supported, not proven.

**Verdict:** the machinery is correct and validated. A *random* rotation is redundant in
this codebase. Whether a *learned* `M` pays is untested — `learn_r1_dump` is still bound
to `load_llama_fp32` and needs porting to the hybrid loader.

Two further costs worth knowing: rotation forces a tied model to **untie** (the head
needs `final_norm` folded, the embed does not), taking embed+head from 254.3M shared to
508.6M separate parameters — **+34%** — and the synthesized head is stored F16
unconditionally. And **norm folding is itself bit-width-dependent**: free at 8 bits
(0.000656 vs 0.000676) but catastrophic at 3 (qtip3 `R1 = I`: 4.370507 vs 0.213695),
because folding `(1+g)` gives readers wildly uneven per-channel scales that an 8-bit grid
absorbs and a 3-bit trellis cannot.

## P2-MTP — the head was never collapsed; it was never reached

Adding an eight-condition diagnostic to the MTP gate named the blocker on the first run:
`KVarN is active`. And it is unavoidable in any supported configuration — the `kv_cache`
enum is `{auto, q8, asym2/3/4, kvarn2, kvarn, kvarn4, kvarn8}`, `q8`/`asym*` are
deprecated and refused, and there is **no unquantized value**. So **MTP spec-decode is
dead by default on qwen3.5.**

Worth a maintainer's decision: DFlash guards with `(!kvarn_active || kvarn_specdecode_ok)`
and that escape is default-on; the MTP route has a bare `!kvarn_active` with no escape.
Not changed here — that is a spec-decode correctness call.

Measured through the deprecated-KV escape (matched KV, `oq4.25++` trunk, `max_n=2`):

| arm | decode tok/s | tau | accept | committed/window |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AR baseline (no head) | 185.70 | — | — | — |
| q8 head, `mtp=off` | 187.00 | — | — | — |
| q8 head (8-bit), `mtp=ON` | 114.70 | 0.608 | 0.304 | 1.608 |
| **mq4 head (4-bit), `mtp=ON`** | **118.70** | **0.641** | **0.321** | **1.641** |

**A 4-bit MTP head does not collapse** — it beats the 8-bit head on every acceptance
metric and is slightly faster, so the 10.4 MiB `mq4` head is the right choice over the
20.7 MiB `q8` one. But MTP is a **net loss at any head width** (~38% slower than AR)
because compact Opus is excluded from batched prefill, so each K-token verify costs K
full weight sweeps. The lever is batched-prefill support for compact Opus, not the
drafter.

## Four silent-failure bugs found and fixed

Every one of these is the same shape: something documented, accepted, and inert.

1. `--embed-precision q8` — a no-op on every bf16-staging format (`q8` shared code 0
   with "unconfigured" = keep the caller's default).
2. `--no-coarse-lmhead` — documented in `--help`, **never parsed** (env var only). That
   tier is +1.36 bpw at a 248320-row vocab.
3. **qwen35 could not load any `oq3` artifact** — `unsupported quant_type 38`. The
   loader arm was `33|35|36|52|54`; `oq8_arch_load_inner` had handled 38 all along, so
   every `oq3` build quantized fine and then failed at load.
4. `--rotate` folding `w` instead of `(1 + w)` on the GemmaRMSNorm family.
5. `--rotate` **silently ignored** with a `.hfq` input — the pre-pass runs only in the
   safetensors pipeline, so the flag was accepted and did nothing (an oq3 arm came out
   byte-identical to its unrotated build). Now refuses.
6. The embedding shipping **unrotated** under `--embed-precision source`, and the
   Hessian never being moved into the rotated frame (both above).

## Corrections to my own earlier conclusions

Recorded because several survived long enough to be written into commit messages:

- *"`--rotate` is imperfect even on its native arch."* Wrong — it came from testing an
  **untied** llama fixture through a different embed path, plus a no-op test that was
  not a no-op (`R1 = FᵀM`, so identity `M` leaves `R1 = Fᵀ`; the real no-op is `M = F`).
- *"An arch-5-specific rotation bug remains."* Wrong — the name table and norm fold were
  already correct; the fault was in the shared source-precision path.
- *"An arbitrary rotation legitimately costs quality."* This was rationalising a result
  that contradicted the literature instead of hunting the bug. Two real bugs were behind
  it. The final explanation (redundant incoherence) is different and testable.
- *"The daemon emits no acceptance telemetry."* Wrong — it emits `tau`, `accept_rate`,
  `acceptance_hist` and more. I saw silence only because the MTP path never executed.
  Absence of output from code that never runs is not absence of the feature.


## Protecting the right tensors — the one lever that beat the trade line

Everything above says sub-4-bit costs ~3.3x the KLD and no reallocation helps. That
conclusion was drawn from *covariance*-ranked protection and it was wrong. Measured on
the same qtip3 body, against the line from interpolating qtip3 → `oq4.25++` (i.e. "would
those bits have been better spent uniformly?"):

| protection | est. body bpw | line predicts | measured | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none (qtip3) | 3.364 | — | 0.213695 | |
| **tensor**, covariance-ranked (M2) | 3.744 | 0.1570 | 0.207588 | **below** |
| **channel** (`roughquant4-sim`, RQ4) | 3.515 | 0.1912 | 0.165100 | above +13.7% |
| **tensor, gamma-ranked (M3)** | 3.535 | 0.1881 | **0.159229** | **above +15.4%** |

Two things follow.

**Ranking, not granularity, was the problem.** `HIPFIRE_MIXED_BPW_RANK` density
(`err_oq4/numel`) is input-covariance only; its top entries here were all
`linear_attn.in_proj_a` — [16,1024] tensors that are 0.158% of the body, so protecting
them is free *and worthless*. The gamma table for the same model puts output projections
(`out_proj`, `down_proj`, `o_proj`) in the entire top 8, exactly as `calib_gamma`'s docs
warn ("ranks `o_proj` 79th-113th of 113 while it is the single largest promotion win").
Re-ranked, the *same* mechanism gives −26% KLD for +10.7 MB and beats even the
channel-protection sim — while being a real packed artifact rather than a sim.

**And the sub-4 artifact now exists.** The blocker was qtip3's post-pass
force-quantizing the embed to `Q8F16` whatever `--embed-precision` said — on exactly the
`.hfq` path where `--tensor-format` lives, so "mixed body + 4-bit embed" was
inexpressible. Fixed with one condition (an explicit `--embed-precision` outranks the Q8
default for the embed *table*; Q8 stays the default and the untied head is unaffected):

| sub-4 arm | MB | bpw | evalA kld | decode | prefill |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| qtip3 + hfq4 embed | 344.5 | 3.66 | 0.246885 | 175.0 | 238 |
| **`Qwen3.5-0.8B-e4--qtip3g.hfq`** | 355.2 | **3.78** | **0.189103** | 173.3 | 233 |

**−23.4% KLD for +3% size**, output coherent, decode unchanged. This is the recommended
sub-4 artifact. It still carries qtip3's ~9× prefill penalty (no `gemm_qtip3`), so
`oq4.25++` + 4-bit embed remains the better choice unless size is the binding constraint.

## Eight accepted-but-inert flags and code paths

The recurring failure mode of this area, all found this session: `--embed-precision q8`
(no-op on bf16-staging formats); `--no-coarse-lmhead` (documented, never parsed); qt 38
missing from the qwen35 loader (every `oq3` build quantized then failed to load);
`--rotate` folding `w` not `(1+w)`; `--rotate` silently ignored on `.hfq` input; the
embed shipping unrotated under `--embed-precision source`; the Hessian never rotated;
`gamma_by_layer` dropping every entry on a wrapped decoder; and `--embed-precision hfq4`
discarded on the `.hfq` path under qtip3. Each parsed, logged as accepted, and did
nothing. When a flag appears to have no effect here, assume it is unwired before
assuming the effect is real.
