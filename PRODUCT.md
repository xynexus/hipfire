# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hipfire's admin console serves operators responsible for a local or remotely
reachable inference service. They need to understand hardware and workload
pressure, provision API access, investigate usage, and contain abuse without
leaving the operational flow or exposing inference content.

## Product Purpose

The console is the trustworthy control plane for hipfire. It makes service
health, API identities, credential lifecycle, effective limits, and usage
history legible and actionable while preserving the separation between admin
access and inference credentials. Success means an operator can safely create,
inspect, limit, disable, and audit access with clear feedback and no ambiguity
about what will happen next.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, technical, trustworthy. The interface should feel calm under pressure,
precise about system state, and confident without becoming theatrical.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS marketing dashboards with decorative hero metrics.
- Cyberpunk control rooms with neon gradients, glass panels, and gratuitous
  animation.
- Card walls that fragment related data into identical floating tiles.
- Destructive actions hidden behind vague icon-only controls or ambiguous copy.
- Dense terminal cosplay that sacrifices hierarchy, accessibility, or familiar
  browser affordances.

## Design Principles

- Put operational truth first: show current state, scope, time range, and
  consequences beside every decision.
- Earn trust through explicit language, predictable controls, and visible audit
  trails.
- Keep the operator in context with progressive detail instead of modal-first
  workflows.
- Use density deliberately: compact enough for expert scanning, spacious enough
  to preserve hierarchy and touch targets.
- Make safe behavior the easy behavior, especially for one-time secrets,
  revocation, user disablement, and limit changes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All workflows must be keyboard complete, use visible focus,
meet text and non-text contrast requirements, expose programmatic labels and
status messages, avoid color-only meaning, honor reduced motion, and remain
usable at narrow mobile widths and browser zoom.
